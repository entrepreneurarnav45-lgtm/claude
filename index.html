Specialist focus

Prosthodontic expertise

Prosthodontics focuses on restoring and replacing damaged or missing teeth—helping rebuild a smile's appearance, comfort and function.

This may include crowns, bridges, dentures, implant-supported restorations and carefully coordinated full-mouth rehabilitation. Dr. Dordi approaches complex needs in clear, patient-friendly steps.

  • Crowns and bridges
  • Implant-supported restorations
  • Complete and partial dentures
  • Coordinated full-mouth rehabilitation
